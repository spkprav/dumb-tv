These are remote photo references, not 50 bundled offline assets.

Each image uses this fixed 16:9 JPEG request: `https://images.pexels.com/photos/ID/pexels-photo-ID.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&fit=crop&w=1920&h=1080`. The exact URLs are allowlisted. No runtime searches or random-image services are used. The bundled lake remains an offline fallback.

The rotation visits the collection without replacement before reshuffling; failed downloads consume an attempt and are retried on later cycles. The disk cache keeps recent approved images within 48 MB and removes obsolete gallery files. Original source records are also in `wallpaper-catalog.json`.
